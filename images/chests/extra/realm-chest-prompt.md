
Create a collection of 10 handcrafted medieval wooden treasure chests inspired by the aesthetic of the Carcassonneboard game. Each chest is shown closed, viewed from a slightly elevated front perspective, standing independently as a collectible board game asset.
These are NOT progression tiers, upgrades, rarity levels, or evolutions. They are 10 different chest designs of equal value and equal prestige, like selecting one of ten handcrafted chest styles made by different medieval craftsmen. None should appear richer, rarer, newer, larger, or more important than another.
Every chest must have the exact same overall size, proportions, silhouette, lid shape, camera angle, perspective, and placement. Think of them as 10 cosmetic variants of the same chest model, where only the artistic details differ.
Vary the designs using combinations of:

different wood grain and wood tones (light oak, honey oak, walnut, weathered oak)
different iron hinge and lock designs
carved geometric patterns
decorative wooden trim
carved borders
engraved panels
painted accents using the classic Carcassonne palette (forest green, royal blue, terracotta, golden yellow)
subtle metal corner reinforcements
different plank layouts
different decorative bands
different medieval craftsmanship styles
Every chest should feel like a premium handcrafted medieval board game component, using clean stylized geometry, beveled edges, simple carved details, and light weathering.
Do not create any visual hierarchy. No chest should look more expensive, more ornate, more legendary, or more luxurious than another. Avoid adding increasing amounts of gold, gemstones, carvings, or decoration across the collection. Instead, distribute decorative elements evenly so every chest feels equally valuable but uniquely designed.
Think of these as 10 skins for the same chest, not 10 levels of a chest.
Arrange the 10 chests in a horizontal row, evenly spaced and perfectly aligned, with each chest isolated so it can be cropped into an individual asset. Use a clean white background, centered composition, soft ambient lighting, subtle shadows, and a consistent art style.
No text, letters, numbers, symbols, characters, hands, coins, treasure, props, or open lids. Only 10 closed medieval wooden chests of equal quality, each with a unique handcrafted design.
The final result should resemble a premium medieval board game icon set, where the player could randomly receive any chest because they are all equally prestigious and purely cosmetic variations of the same item.
