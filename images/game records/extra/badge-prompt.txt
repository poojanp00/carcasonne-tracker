Create a collection of handcrafted medieval wooden achievement badges inspired by the aesthetic of the Carcassonne board game. Each badge is carved from warm oak wood with subtle painted accents in the classic Carcassonne color palette (forest green, royal blue, terracotta, golden yellow). The badges should look like premium board game components rather than realistic objects. Use simple geometric shapes, clean silhouettes, beveled edges, engraved details, and light weathering. Each badge should have a transparent background, centered composition, soft ambient lighting, and no text or numbers.

A carved wooden shield with a winding road engraved through the center (Longest Road)

A carved wooden shield featuring a large medieval city with towers and walls (Largest City)

A carved wooden shield with rolling farmland divided into fields (Largest Field)

A carved wooden shield with a monastery surrounded by a garden (Largest Monastery)

A carved wooden shield with a pennant banner and laurel wreath (Winner)

A carved wooden shield with a trophy cup and small crown (Game Record)
